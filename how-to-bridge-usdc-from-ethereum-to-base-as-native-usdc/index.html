How to bridge USDC from Ethereum to Base as native USDC

How to bridge USDC from Ethereum to Base: use Circle CCTP to burn USDC on Ethereum and mint native USDC on Base, then check the token contract in your wallet.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 04:04 UTC3 min read

A CCTP-based bridge moves USDC from Ethereum to Base as native USDC, so you have the token most Base apps accept. Follow these steps to complete one transfer and confirm the token in your wallet.

Native USDC works with Base apps that accept it

Circle CCTP burns USDC on Ethereum and mints native USDC on Base. USDbC is an earlier bridged version of USDC on Base; it is a separate token. A vault listing native USDC will not take USDbC, and a vault listing USDbC will not take native USDC.

The Beefy app is a multichain yield optimizer that automatically compounds rewards from supported strategies. That feature makes it a useful example in our article about cheap Arbitrum bridging for Beefy vaults.

Bridge USDC from Ethereum to Base in six steps

  1. Open a bridge that routes USDC through Circle CCTP. Connect your wallet, with Base added as a network.
  2. Select Ethereum as the source, Base as the destination, and USDC as the token. Enter the amount to bridge.
  3. Approve the USDC spend. This lets the bridge contract use the amount you selected.
  4. Confirm the bridge transaction in your wallet. Keep ETH on Ethereum to pay gas for the approval and bridge transaction.
  5. Claim the USDC on Base if the bridge prompts you after the CCTP attestation, the confirmation that the source transfer is ready to complete. Some routes need this second transaction. Keep a little ETH on Base for the claim and later transactions.
  6. Check that your wallet shows native USDC on Base. If the balance is hidden, add the native USDC contract for Base to your wallet and check the token details. Confirm the contract matches native USDC, not USDbC.

Compared with sending funds through an exchange, this route means paying Ethereum gas once and skipping the exchange’s deposit and withdrawal waits. Once native USDC arrives, a Beefy app USDC vault on Base can take it if the vault lists that token.

This route covers Ethereum to Base. A return transfer using a rollup-native withdrawal from Base to Ethereum has a roughly seven-day challenge period, so that timing does not apply to this CCTP transfer in the other direction.
