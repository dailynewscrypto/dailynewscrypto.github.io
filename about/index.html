About Crypto Dispatch

We report on the market mechanics behind crypto infrastructure: liquidity provision, transaction ordering, collateral, and settlement. Our stories follow capital and bargaining power through the stack, explaining what changes for developers choosing venues, designing incentives, or integrating payment rails.

We make editorial decisions independently of the projects we cover. We source reporting from protocol documentation, code, governance records, onchain data, and named interviews. We distinguish observed flows from inferred positioning and disclose relevant conflicts. We correct factual errors promptly and append dated notes explaining substantive changes.
