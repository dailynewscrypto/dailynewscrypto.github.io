WETH After a Swap: Your ETH Is Still Worth 1:1

Received WETH instead of ETH after a swap? WETH is ETH wrapped as a token and usually keeps its 1:1 value; unwrap it on Ethereum or an L2 to get native ETH.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomNov 20, 2026 · 13:57 UTC2 min read

WETH after a swap usually means WETH was selected as the buy token, or the swap ran on a chain where ETH exists as a token; it is redeemable 1:1 for ETH on Ethereum and most L2s.

Why did my swap return WETH?

ETH is the native asset on Ethereum, while WETH is ETH wrapped as an ERC-20 token, a format that smart contracts can use. During a swap, your input token goes through a route across liquidity sources, and the selected buy token comes out. If that token is WETH, your wallet displays WETH.

  • Input: the token you traded away.
  • Route: the liquidity sources used to make the exchange.
  • Output: the buy token you selected, such as WETH.

Matcha swap lists both ETH and WETH in token search, so it is possible to choose WETH by mistake. If you are also checking why the trade cost more than expected, read our breakdown of Matcha swap fees; Matcha swap fees can be traced separately from the token you received.

Take a swap of 1,000 USDC to WETH on Ethereum. Your wallet will show WETH, not ETH, but the WETH represents the same amount of ETH at a 1:1 rate. The value is intact; only the token format differs.

How do I turn WETH into ETH?

On Ethereum, unwrap WETH to receive native ETH. The WETH contract’s withdraw function redeems WETH 1:1. Matcha swap also lets you swap WETH to ETH, so select WETH as the sell token and ETH as the buy token, then review the trade before confirming.

WETH exists because DeFi contracts work with ERC-20 tokens, while ETH is the network’s native asset. Wrapping puts ETH into a token format contracts can handle; withdrawing reverses that process. Wallets may also offer an unwrap action for WETH.

Check the network shown in your wallet before trying to unwrap. On Polygon, the native gas token is POL, not ETH. ETH on Polygon exists as bridged WETH, so there is no native ETH on that chain to receive by unwrapping. To get native ETH, bridge the WETH to Ethereum or an L2 that supports native ETH, then unwrap it there.

WETH can be useful when a DeFi app or contract requires an ERC-20 token. For ordinary ETH payments or gas on Ethereum, use native ETH instead. On Polygon, POL pays gas; WETH remains the token form of bridged ETH.
