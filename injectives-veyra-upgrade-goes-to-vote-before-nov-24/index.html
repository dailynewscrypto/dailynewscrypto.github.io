Injective’s Veyra upgrade goes to vote before Nov. 24

Injective’s Veyra upgrade is up for a governance vote, with a Nov. 24 target that would bring new cross-chain and institutional trading tools to the network.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomNov 13, 2026 · 11:56 UTC2 min read

Injective has opened a governance vote on its Veyra mainnet upgrade, which would add cross-chain and institutional trading tools if approved. Injective’s upgrade tracker lists proposal #717 as voting, with a planned network halt at block 193,145,000. The halt is targeted for Nov. 24, 2026.

The vote covers a switch to software version 1.21.0

The proposal would move Injective to version 1.21.0. It was submitted on Nov. 12 and voting is scheduled to close on Nov. 16 at 4:53 p.m., according to the Injective governance proposal. The proposal page showed that the vote had not yet reached its 33.4% quorum, the minimum share of eligible voting power required for a decision. Its listed result was still undecided.

If the proposal passes, the chain would pause at the stated block height and resume using the updated software. That makes the Nov. 24 date conditional on approval, rather than a confirmed launch.

Veyra would add cross-chain and trading features

The proposal says Veyra would expand Injective’s MultiVM system with Inter-Blockchain Communication (IBC), a protocol for moving data and assets between connected blockchains. It describes the change as enabling assets to move between Injective’s Ethereum Virtual Machine environment and the wider interchain ecosystem.

Veyra would also add request-for-quote infrastructure, which lets traders ask liquidity providers for a price, and expand atomic swaps—asset exchanges designed to complete together or not at all. The proposal says these tools are intended to support institutional trading and settlement involving tokenized assets.

Privacy work remains part of a future roadmap

The proposal links Veyra to CypherOS, Injective’s planned native privacy platform for confidential trading and financial transactions. It describes Veyra as preparing infrastructure for that roadmap; it does not say CypherOS will launch with this upgrade.

The proposal also says Veyra builds on Injective Institutional Rail, a recently launched service mentioned in the upgrade plan. Together, the proposed changes focus on moving tokenized assets between networks and connecting them with trading and settlement tools. The governance vote will decide whether Injective proceeds with the software upgrade on the proposed schedule.
