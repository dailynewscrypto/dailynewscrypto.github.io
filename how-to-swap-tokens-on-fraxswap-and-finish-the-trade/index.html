How to swap tokens on Fraxswap and finish the trade

How to swap tokens on Fraxswap: connect your wallet, match the network, choose tokens, set slippage, approve if needed, then confirm and check the result.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomNov 16, 2026 · 20:34 UTC3 min read

Use Fraxswap to choose the tokens and amount, approve the token if prompted, and confirm the swap in your wallet. The trade runs against on-chain liquidity pools, so your wallet needs to be connected to the chain holding your tokens.

  • Connect a self-custody wallet with the chain’s native gas token and the token you want to sell.
  • Match the app’s network selector to the chain holding those tokens.
  • Choose the tokens and amount, set slippage, approve if prompted, and confirm.
  • Look for the new balance in your wallet and a successful transaction on the block explorer.

Fraxswap: make a standard token swap

Open the official Frax app and go to its Fraxswap section. Use Fraxswap at this step to make the token trade from your wallet against the available on-chain pools.

Before you start, have a self-custody wallet, the token you want to sell, and the network’s native gas token. The gas token pays for transactions on that chain. The app supports several chains, including Ethereum, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Optimism and Fraxtal.

  1. Connect your wallet. Connect the wallet that holds the tokens you plan to trade.
  2. Select the network. Set the app’s network selector to the chain where your tokens are held. Say your tokens are on Arbitrum: choose Arbitrum, not Ethereum. Balances on another chain won’t appear, and the swap button stays disabled until the selected chain matches. Make sure your wallet also has that chain’s native gas token.
  3. Choose the sell and buy tokens. Select the token you’re giving and the one you want to receive. Confirm that you picked the intended tokens before moving on.
  4. Enter the amount. Type how much of the sell token you want to trade. Review the amount and the selected pair.
  5. Set slippage tolerance. Slippage is how far the price can move before the swap reverts. Keep the tolerance low for liquid pairs. A higher tolerance allows a larger price change before the transaction fails.
  6. Approve the token if prompted. The first swap of an ERC-20 token requires a separate approval transaction. Approve it in your wallet, then return to the swap. Approval is generally one-time per token; the swap itself is a second transaction.
  7. Confirm the swap. Review the transaction in your wallet and confirm it. Then check that the new token balance appears in your wallet and that the transaction is marked successful on the block explorer. Once it is confirmed, you can use the received tokens from that wallet.

Questions readers ask next

Why did my Fraxswap transaction fail?

A transaction can fail if the selected network doesn’t hold the tokens, the wallet lacks gas, or the price moves beyond your slippage tolerance before execution. Check the network and gas balance, then review the pair and tolerance before trying again.

How do I add a token that doesn’t appear in the Fraxswap list?

Use the token’s contract address to identify the correct asset, and confirm that it belongs to the network selected in the app. Token availability depends on the selected chain; a token on another network won’t appear as a balance for this swap.
