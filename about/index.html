About Crypto Daybook

Crypto Daybook reports news from across crypto: Bitcoin, Ethereum and other networks, exchanges, stablecoins, DeFi, regulation, security incidents and the companies building in the space. We cover many projects and are not tied to any of them.

We work from primary sources where we can — filings, official statements, on-chain data and court records — and we say where each claim comes from. When we get something wrong, we fix it and note the correction on the story. Crypto Daybook is independent, and nothing we publish is financial advice.
