Weekly vs monthly staking claims: monthly usually saves more

Claim staking rewards weekly or monthly? Monthly usually saves more on Ethereum mainnet because each claim has a fee, while weekly claims add extra gas costs.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 02:18 UTC3 min read

Choose monthly claims if you want to spend less on gas and do not restake rewards. Each claim has a fee that is roughly fixed for the same claim route, so weekly claims pay about four fees for the rewards one monthly claim collects.

In Aave staking, the Aave Rewards Controller keeps accrued rewards claimable until you claim them. For background on Aave staking claim frequency, see how the timing choice fits the wider staking process.

Will you restake the rewards sooner?

Monthly claims usually save more when you keep rewards separate, because claiming them weekly adds fees without increasing the amount you earn from restaking.

Weekly claims can add compounding when you put rewards back to work as soon as they are claimed. But that extra return has to outweigh the fees for the additional claims. On Ethereum, where gas can be costly, waiting a month often leaves more value in your pocket.

The trade-off has an approximate break-even interval: √(2c ÷ (P × r²)) years, or that result multiplied by 365 for days. Here, c is the cost of one claim, P is the value of your stake, and r is the APR written as a decimal. The estimate balances the cost of claiming against the compounding gained by reinvesting sooner.

As the stake or APR rises, the interval gets shorter, so more frequent claims can make sense. As claim costs rise, it gets longer. Treat the result as a guide: actual gas prices change, and claim costs can vary with the transaction.

Is the claim fee small compared with your stake?

A large stake can earn enough extra through earlier restaking to cover more frequent fees; a small stake may not. Claim cost depends on gas used and the network’s gas price, rather than the reward amount. Etherscan’s Gas Tracker shows current Ethereum gas prices, which help estimate a claim’s cost.

For the weekly-versus-monthly choice, compare the cost of roughly three extra weekly claims with the added return from restaking earlier. On a cheap L2, those fees may be low enough for weekly claims to win. On Ethereum mainnet, high gas costs push the choice toward monthly, unless the stake and APR make the compounding benefit large enough.

Choose weekly when you regularly restake, your stake is large relative to the claim fee, or you claim on a low-cost L2. Choose monthly when rewards simply accrue, your stake is modest, or gas is high.

Do you put each claim back into your stake, and what does one claim cost on your network? Those answers show whether monthly is the cheaper default or whether earlier restaking is likely to justify weekly fees.
