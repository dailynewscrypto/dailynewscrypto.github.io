About Web3 News

Web3 News reports news from across crypto: markets and exchanges, blockchain networks and their upgrades, stablecoins, decentralized finance, security incidents and the rules governments write for all of it. No single project, token or company sets the agenda; every story is chosen on its news value.

We work from primary sources such as filings, official announcements, on-chain records and project documentation, and we name them in the story. When we get something wrong, we correct the article and note the change. Web3 News is independent, and nothing published here is financial advice.
