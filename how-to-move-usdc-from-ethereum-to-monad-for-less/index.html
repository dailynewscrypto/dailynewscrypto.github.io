How to Move USDC from Ethereum to Monad for Less

Cheapest way to move USDC from Ethereum to Monad: compare native USDC and permit routes, then send at a low Ethereum base fee to cut total cost.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomNov 20, 2026 · 01:59 UTC3 min read

Ethereum gas usually sets most of the cost of moving USDC to Monad. To find the cheapest route, compare the total cost for the same amount and avoid extra Ethereum transactions.

Start with the Monad bridge and compare its quote with Across, Stargate where it offers the route, and Circle’s USDC bridge. The monad mainnet bridge can help you reach the Monad bridge option as you compare routes.

Compare total cost for the same amount

Use the amount you plan to send and compare what each route says you will pay and receive. Total cost is Ethereum gas (base fee × gas used), plus the route fee and any swap spread.

  1. Open each route with USDC on Ethereum as the source and Monad as the destination. Keep the amount and recipient address the same so the quotes are comparable.
  2. Choose a route that delivers native USDC on Monad. Circle’s CCTP burns USDC on Ethereum and mints native USDC on Monad, so you avoid a swap on arrival. A cheaper-looking quote can cost more if it delivers a different token and you need to swap it.
  3. Check whether the route accepts an EIP-2612 permit signature. USDC on Ethereum supports permits: you sign a token allowance instead of sending a separate approve transaction. That can save Ethereum gas. If a route does not offer a permit, an existing approval for that route may also avoid a new approval transaction; confirm the token, spender, and allowance in your wallet.
  4. Send when Ethereum’s base fee is low, after checking the quote again. Say the quote looks best, but the wallet asks for a separate approval: include that transaction’s gas in the total before deciding. Keep ETH available for Ethereum gas and a small MON balance, or a gas drop-off option, for activity on Monad.
  5. Confirm the USDC balance at your address on a Monad explorer. The transfer is complete when the balance appears.

Use the quote that avoids extra gas and swaps

The lowest all-in cost depends on the amount, route fee, approval state, and Ethereum base fee when you send. For a routine move, favor a native-USDC route with a permit or reusable approval, then compare its final quote at a low base fee. Monad-side costs are usually small beside Ethereum gas, but include them in the quote.

Related questions

Is bridged USDC on Monad the same as native USDC?

Native USDC is issued on Monad by Circle. CCTP burns native USDC on Ethereum and mints native USDC on Monad. Other routes may deliver a bridged version, so check the destination token before sending.

How long does USDC take to reach Monad?

Timing depends on the route and Ethereum confirmation conditions. Read the route’s current estimate before signing; a faster option may have a different fee.

For the cheapest move, choose the quote with the lowest total cost after gas, route fees, and any spread. Ethereum gas is usually the deciding expense, so avoiding an extra approval and sending at a low base fee can make the biggest difference.
