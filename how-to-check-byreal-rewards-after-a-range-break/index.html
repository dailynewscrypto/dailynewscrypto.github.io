How to Check Byreal Rewards After a Range Break

When a pool price moves outside your range, check your position, eligible rewards and claim status separately to see what stopped earning and what you can collect.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomOct 24, 2026 · 02:16 UTC3 min read

If a pool price moves outside your range, your Byreal position may stop earning trading fees until the price returns, so check its status before deciding what to do. A range break means the market price has moved above or below the band you chose for your liquidity. The tokens in your position can shift toward one asset as the price moves.

Fees and incentive rewards are separate. Fees come from swaps through the pool; incentives depend on the rules for that reward program. For more on how Byreal swaps, liquidity fees and outcome markets work, read this guide to Byreal swaps and LP fees. Then check the terms for the specific pool and reward campaign you joined.

What changes when a pool price leaves your range?

When the price is outside your selected band, your liquidity is no longer active for trades at the current price. That usually means it stops earning swap fees while it remains out of range. It does not mean the position or its past earnings have vanished. The position may hold mostly one of its two tokens, and its value can change with the market.

Check the pool’s current price against both ends of your range. Then open your position and compare its token amounts with what you deposited. If the price is still outside the band, fee earnings may resume only if it moves back in. You can leave the position in place, adjust its range or withdraw liquidity; each choice changes your exposure and may involve transaction costs.

Where can you check Byreal rewards?

Start with the connected wallet and the position for the pool in question. Look for the position’s fee balance and any separate reward balance. A displayed estimate is useful, but the pool’s eligibility rules decide whether incentives continue while the price is out of range. Do not treat a fee pause as proof that every reward has stopped, or assume incentives keep accruing.

Check these details before you claim or change the position:

  • The pool and wallet match the position you mean to inspect.
  • The current price is above, inside or below your chosen range.
  • Fees and campaign rewards appear as separate amounts, if both apply.
  • The reward terms explain eligibility, timing and whether a claim is available.

How do you confirm a reward is claimable?

Use the app’s reward or position view to check whether the amount is pending or available to claim. If the display seems stale, refresh it and compare the wallet’s activity with the relevant on-chain transaction records. A reward estimate is not the same as a completed claim. After claiming, confirm that the transaction succeeded and that the wallet received the token.

Before signing, check the token, amount and transaction details in your wallet. A narrow range can put capital to work efficiently while the market stays inside it, but it also needs closer monitoring. For most readers, the useful first step after a break is to verify eligibility and balances, then decide whether the range still fits their plan.
