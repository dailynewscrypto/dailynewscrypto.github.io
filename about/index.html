About Web3 News

Web3 News is an independent newsroom covering the crypto world as news: blockchains, tokens, exchanges, regulation, security incidents and the groups building and policing them. We write for readers who are new to the subject, so every technical term is explained in plain words the first time it appears.

We report from primary sources such as filings, official statements, code repositories and public blockchain records, and we say clearly when something is unconfirmed. When we get something wrong, we correct the article and note the change. No project pays for coverage. We are independent, and nothing we publish is financial advice.
