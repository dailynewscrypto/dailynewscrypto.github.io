Editorial policy

Our coverage

Our beat is Crypto News — exchanges, blockchains and protocols, policy and regulation, security incidents and the companies building in the space. We cover developments; we do not offer trading calls or price predictions.

How we source stories

We work from primary material wherever it exists — company and project statements, regulatory documents, on-chain records — and from reputable reporting. Sources are linked in the text so readers can check them.

Independence

Our stories are written for readers, not for the projects they describe. A reference to any token, platform or service is information, not a recommendation.

Fixing mistakes

Mistakes can happen in fast-moving news. When we find an error of fact, we correct the published article.

Investment risk

Nothing we publish is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset. Crypto markets are volatile, and readers should make their own decisions and seek professional advice where needed.
