What do I need before my first lending vault deposit? A checklist

Before your first lending vault deposit, have a backed-up wallet, the right asset and gas on its chain, and the vault address ready to deposit in one sitting.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomNov 28, 2026 · 00:08 UTC3 min read

Before your first Morpho vault deposit, have a self-custody wallet with its recovery phrase backed up offline, the vault’s asset on the right chain, native gas for transactions, and the chosen vault’s address. A deposit usually takes two on-chain actions from your wallet: approve the asset, then deposit it.

  • A wallet you control, with its recovery phrase backed up offline.
  • The vault’s exact asset on its chain.
  • Some of that chain’s native token for gas.
  • A chosen vault, its curator’s name, and its contract address.

Prepare these before you deposit

These checks make sure your wallet can sign both transactions and has the right funds to complete them.

  • Set up your wallet and recovery phrase. An exchange account cannot sign a vault deposit; the funds must be in a self-custody wallet. Write the recovery phrase on paper and store it somewhere private and secure. Never share it or enter it into a website. You are ready when you can open the wallet and see its address without needing to look up the phrase online.
  • Match the asset and network. Vaults accept a particular asset on a particular chain. A USDC balance on Ethereum, for instance, is not the same as a USDC balance on Base or Arbitrum for this transaction. Check the selected vault’s asset and chain, then confirm the wallet shows that asset on that same chain before moving funds.
  • Keep gas for two transactions. The approval and deposit each need a transaction fee paid in the chain’s native token. The amount varies with network conditions and the chain, so leave a little extra rather than using your entire balance for the deposit. You are ready when the wallet shows the correct gas token and enough to submit both actions.
  • Choose the vault and save its address. Note the vault’s asset, chain, curator (the party that sets its strategy), and contract address. Morpho vaults use ERC-4626, a standard for tokenized vault shares. A short background on Morpho vaults explains how funds can be allocated across lending markets and other supported yield sources; the address identifies the specific vault you chose.
  • Keep a way to find the contract again. Save the address in a private note, along with the chain and the vault name. If the app is unavailable, you can use the block explorer for that chain: open the verified vault contract and its Write Contract tab to call redeem. Confirm the address and network before interacting. This gives you an exit route that does not depend on one website.

Related

How much should my first vault deposit be?

Your first deposit should be an amount you can afford to leave in the vault and are comfortable managing on-chain. Start with a smaller amount if you want to learn the approval and deposit steps before committing more. Keep enough of the asset for the deposit and enough native token for both fees.

Can I deposit straight from an exchange account?

No. An exchange account cannot sign the approval and deposit transactions for a vault. Withdraw the chosen asset to your self-custody wallet on the vault’s chain, then check that it arrived before you connect the wallet. Once the wallet, funds, gas, and saved address are ready, open the chosen vault and review the transaction details before approving; that lets you complete the deposit in one sitting.
