About Crypto Market Dispatch

Crypto Market Dispatch is an independent newsroom covering digital asset markets, exchange flows, protocols and regulation. We explain the mechanics behind each development so active traders can distinguish a meaningful change from a passing headline.

Our reporting prioritizes primary documents, verifiable data and clearly attributed sources. We distinguish evidence from interpretation, disclose relevant conflicts and keep editorial decisions independent of commercial relationships. We correct material errors promptly and explain what changed. Coverage is journalism, not a recommendation to trade.
