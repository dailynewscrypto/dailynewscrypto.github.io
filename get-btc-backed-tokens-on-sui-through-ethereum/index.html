Get BTC-backed tokens on Sui through Ethereum

To get Bitcoin onto the Sui network, buy WBTC or LBTC on Ethereum and bridge it to your Sui wallet with Suibridge; native BTC cannot move directly.

Crypto Daybook NewsroomNov 25, 2026 · 14:12 UTC3 min read

To get Bitcoin exposure onto Sui, first hold WBTC or LBTC on Ethereum, because Suibridge transfers supported Ethereum assets rather than native BTC. The Ethereum token represents your BTC exposure, and the Sui coin you receive is backed by tokens held in the Suibridge vault.

Bitcoin has no smart contracts for a bridge to read, so it cannot move directly through this route. Before using Suibridge, confirm that the Ethereum token you plan to deposit is the one you want to hold on Sui, and note its issuer and backing.

Prepare both wallets and an Ethereum token

Have WBTC or LBTC in an Ethereum wallet, ETH in that wallet for gas, your Sui wallet address, and a small SUI balance for later transactions. WBTC is minted against BTC held in BitGo custody. Only approved merchants can mint WBTC directly from BTC, so most holders will need to buy it on an exchange or swap for it on Ethereum. LBTC is also supported from Ethereum; check its issuer’s information to understand its backing.

  1. Acquire WBTC or LBTC on Ethereum. Buy the token on an exchange or swap for it using your Ethereum wallet. You should see the token in that wallet before you start.
  2. Connect your Ethereum wallet to Suibridge. The connected wallet should show the token you plan to transfer.
  3. Approve the token. Confirm the approval in your Ethereum wallet. This allows the bridge to use the amount you choose to deposit.
  4. Enter your Sui recipient address and amount, then deposit. Use your Sui wallet’s 64-character hexadecimal address and review both fields before confirming. Your Ethereum wallet should show the deposit transaction.
  5. Wait for Ethereum finality and validator signing. The transfer completes after the Ethereum deposit reaches finality and validators sign it. Then check your Sui wallet for the BTC-backed coin under the Suibridge coin type; that confirms the task is done.

Say you want BTC exposure for a Sui DeFi position. This route gives you a BTC-backed coin in your Sui wallet, but the Sui coin is backed by tokens in the bridge vault, which are backed by custodied BTC. Keep the coin type and issuer details handy when checking whether a Sui app accepts it.

Related questions

Is WBTC or LBTC better to bridge to Sui?

Both are supported from Ethereum. WBTC has a named custody detail: BitGo holds the BTC against which it is minted. Choose based on the backing and issuer information you can verify, and confirm that the Sui app you plan to use accepts the resulting coin type.

Can I bridge BTC to Sui without going through Ethereum?

Not through this Suibridge route. It transfers supported Ethereum assets to Sui, so you first need WBTC or LBTC on Ethereum.

Before using the bridged coin

How do I redeem bridged WBTC back to BTC?

First check whether a supported route can return the Sui coin to Ethereum. Then check the current WBTC redemption options: direct minting from BTC is limited to approved merchants, so ordinary holders should confirm their exchange or other redemption route before bridging.

Which Sui protocols accept bridged WBTC as collateral?

Check each protocol’s supported collateral list for the exact Suibridge coin type. A token with the same ticker may have a different coin type and may not be accepted.

What happens to LBTC staking rewards after bridging to Sui?

Do not assume rewards continue after bridging. Check LBTC’s issuer information to see how rewards are handled and whether the bridged coin reflects them before using it in a Sui position.
